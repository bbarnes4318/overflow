Questions

What agency owners ask before they fund a campaign.

Are these agents actually licensed?

Every producer is checked before they reach you: an active resident license with a health line of authority, the non-resident licenses in the states you need, the National Producer Number matched to the license record, marketplace registration, and the carrier appointments they already hold. You get it in writing.

Where do these agents come from?

They are already selling. Most are between agencies, unhappy with their split, or writing other lines and want marketplace business this season. We reach them directly through our own outbound messaging platform, not job boards.

What if an agent isn't marketplace-registered yet?

We confirm registration for the plan year, or the date the producer will complete it, before you talk to them. You decide whether that date works for your season.

Can you recruit in specific states only?

Yes. You set the states, including the non-resident licenses you need covered, and we recruit to that list.

What if you can't find my agents?

We keep recruiting until your count is filled. The campaign is measured in producers signed to your agency, so it isn't finished until they are.

What if an agent quits in the first month?

If they are not still with you thirty days after signing, tell us. That producer comes off your count and we recruit a replacement at no charge.

Why do I pay before you deliver?

Recruiting starts the day the campaign is funded. The outreach, the license checks and the screening all happen before the first producer reaches your phone. The thirty-day replacement is what protects you after they do.

Is there a minimum?

Yes. Campaigns start at five producers: $500 each from five to nine, $450 each at ten or more. There are no other fees.

How fast does it start?

Campaigns start once we have agreed your standard — states, compensation model, carrier lines and roster size — and the campaign is funded. Nothing runs before both.

Do you recruit for Medicare or final expense as well?

Yes. The process is the same; the license and appointment checks change with the line.

Do you keep any piece of what the agent writes?

No. The producer signs under your agency, on your contract. The business is yours. The per-producer rate is the only charge.

What happens after I send the form?

We reply within one business day with the schedule and the price for the count you asked for. If it is a fit, we agree the standard in writing and the campaign starts when it is funded. If it isn't, we will say so.
