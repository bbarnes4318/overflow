08

Questions.

Before you fund a campaign
Which lines do you recruit for?
Medicare, Final Expense and ACA Health.The process is the same for each; the license, certification and appointment checks change with the line.
Do I have to interview them?
No.We interview, hire and complete the paperwork against the standard you set. Producers arrive signed to your agency and ready to write.
Are these producers actually licensed?
Yes, and you get it in writing.Active resident license with the right line of authority, the non-resident licenses you need, the NPN matched to the license record, the line's credentials, and the appointments they already hold.
Where do they come from?
They are already selling. Most are between agencies, unhappy with their split, or writing one line and want to add another. We reach them through our own outbound messaging platform, not job boards.
Can you recruit in specific states only?
Yes.You set the states, including the non-resident licenses you need covered, and we recruit to that list.
What if you can't find my producers?
We keep recruiting until your count is filled. The campaign is measured in producers signed to your agency, so it isn't finished until they are.
What if one quits in the first month?
We replace them.Not still with you thirty days after signing? Tell us. That producer comes off your count and we recruit a replacement at no charge.
Why do I pay before you deliver?
Recruiting starts the day the campaign is funded. The outreach, the license checks and the screening all happen before the first producer reaches your phone. The thirty-day replacement protects you after they do.
Is there a minimum?
Five producers.$500 each from five to nine, $450 each at ten or more. There are no other fees.
Do you keep any of what they write?
No.The producer is contracted under your agency, on your paper. The business is yours. The per-producer rate is the only charge.
What happens after I send the form?
We reply within one business day with the schedule and the price for the count you asked for. If it's a fit, we agree the standard in writing and the campaign starts when it's funded. If it isn't, we'll say so.
