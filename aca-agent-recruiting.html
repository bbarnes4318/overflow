Questions

What agency owners ask before they fund a campaign.

Which lines do you recruit for?
Medicare, Final Expense and ACA Health.

The process is the same for each; the license, certification and appointment checks change with the line.

Are these producers actually licensed?
Yes, and you get it in writing.

Every producer is checked before they reach you: an active resident license with the right line of authority, the non-resident licenses in the states you need, the National Producer Number matched to the license record, the line-specific credentials, and the carrier appointments they already hold.

Where do these producers come from?

They are already selling. Most are between agencies, unhappy with their split, or writing one line and want to add another. We reach them directly through our own outbound messaging platform, not job boards.

Can you recruit in specific states only?
Yes.

You set the states, including the non-resident licenses you need covered, and we recruit to that list.

What if you can't find my producers?

We keep recruiting until your count is filled. The campaign is measured in producers signed to your agency, so it isn't finished until they are.

What if a producer quits in the first month?
We replace them.

If they are not still with you thirty days after signing, tell us. That producer comes off your count and we recruit a replacement at no charge.

Why do I pay before you deliver?

Recruiting starts the day the campaign is funded. The outreach, the license checks and the screening all happen before the first producer reaches your phone. The thirty-day replacement is what protects you after they do.

Is there a minimum?
Five producers.

$500 each from five to nine, $450 each at ten or more. There are no other fees.

Do you keep any piece of what the producer writes?
No.

The producer signs under your agency, on your contract. The business is yours. The per-producer rate is the only charge.

What happens after I send the form?

We reply within one business day with the schedule and the price for the count you asked for. If it is a fit, we agree the standard in writing and the campaign starts when it is funded. If it isn't, we will say so.
