01 — The problem

The problem isn't finding another lead.

Every producer can buy volume. What's hard to buy is a conversation with a real chance of becoming an application — and a price that only comes due when it does.

Stale
The prospect is already spoken for.

By the time you dial an internet lead, it has been sold to three other agents and the consumer has stopped answering.

Intent
They filled out a form. They didn't ask for a policy.

Incentivised forms and co-registration paths produce names, not buyers. You find out which is which on your own time.

Contact
Half the list never picks up. You paid for all of it.

Contactability is the cost nobody prices. A lead you can't reach is a lead you bought.

Dialing
Your best closer spends the day as a phone bank.

Every hour spent chasing a callback is an hour not spent on a presentation.

Incentive
The vendor gets paid when the lead is delivered.

Whether it converts is your problem. That is the whole economics of volume pricing, and it is why quality drifts.

NetEnroll is built around what happens after the opportunity arrives. The consumer is on the line, the producer is licensed in that state, and the only invoice is for the applications that get written.

06 — Questions

What producers ask before they fund the first application.

What am I paying for?

A submitted application. A charge is logged only when your producer completes and submits a carrier application for a consumer NetEnroll delivered. Delivered call time, non-qualifying prospects and dropped calls carry a $0 charge.

How does the application model work?

You fund applications in advance through Stripe, starting with a single one. Live consumers are routed to your producers in the states you selected. When an application is submitted, one is drawn from your balance. Buy more whenever you want them. There is no subscription.

What happens after I sign up?

Create your account at agents.netenroll.com, choose the states you are licensed to write in, choose your programs, and fund your first application. Routing starts once your account is configured and your states are mapped.

Who is this designed for?

Licensed final expense producers who would rather present than prospect, and the agencies and sales organizations that run them. If you are not licensed to write in the states you select, the calls are no use to you.

Can agencies use NetEnroll?

Yes. Register as a company, add your producers, and route by licensed state across the roster. Every application is recorded against the producer and the call it came from.

How does pricing work?

$199 per submitted final expense application, $125 for Medicare, $100 for ACA. No setup fee, no monthly platform fee, no minimum purchase and no cancellation penalty. The price is per application, not "from".

Where do I access my agent tools?

At agents.netenroll.com. That is where your states, programs, call log, applications and billing live. This site holds no session and never collects a credential.

What happens if I need help?
