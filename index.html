Questions

You probably have questions. Here are the answers.

Do I pay for the call?
No.

NetEnroll does not charge you for connecting the consumer, the length of the conversation or a call that doesn't result in an application.

You pay the published program rate when a qualifying application is submitted.

What if the consumer says no?
You pay $0.

That's the point.

What if the consumer isn't qualified?
You pay $0.

A conversation that doesn't produce a qualifying submitted application does not become a call charge.

Is there a monthly fee?
No.

There is no monthly subscription or per-producer seat fee.

What are the rates?

Final Expense: $199 per submitted application

Medicare: $159 per submitted application

ACA Health: $100 per submitted application

Those are the starting rates at an assumed 10% conversion. After the testing period your rate is reassessed against your actual conversion. See the full rate-by-conversion table.

Where do the consumers come from?

Consumers call looking for insurance coverage. NetEnroll routes those live conversations to available producers based on the programs and states you've enabled.

Can I choose my states?
Yes.

You control the programs and states your account is set up to receive.

Can I use NetEnroll for my entire agency?
Yes.

Agency accounts can have multiple producers operating under the account, subject to the applicable program requirements.

Do you guarantee a certain number of calls?
No.

Consumer demand varies by program, state and time.

What we can tell you upfront is what you will pay when an application is submitted.

What happens if a policy doesn't take effect?

If an application qualifies under the applicable replacement terms, NetEnroll provides the corresponding replacement application according to those terms. See Terms §5.5

How do I get started?

Create your producer account, select the programs and states you want, and get your team ready to take calls.
