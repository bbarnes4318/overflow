Questions
agents ask.

If you are evaluating NetEnroll for yourself or an agency, here is the short version.

What exactly do I pay for?

You pay the published program rate when a submitted application is recorded. The current published rates on this page are $199 for Final Expense, $125 for Medicare and $100 for ACA Health.

Do I pay for a call that does not result in an application?

No. The central model is application-based. If the call does not produce a submitted application, there is no application charge for that opportunity.

What insurance programs are available?

NetEnroll currently offers Final Expense, Medicare and ACA Health Insurance calls. Availability can depend on producer licensing and the programs enabled on your account.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No monthly software subscription is required for the core call program.

How do I get started?

Start your account through the Get Started button. Once your account is configured, you can manage the programs and producer setup through the agent portal.
