Before you sign up

Straight answers.

The things agents and agency owners ask us before they open an account.

I really pay nothing if the call doesn't turn into an application?
Nothing. Not for the call, not for the minutes, not for the consumer who says no. A call without a submitted application never appears on your bill.
What exactly triggers a charge?
A submitted application: one your producer completes and submits for a consumer who came in on a NetEnroll call, recorded against your account with the carrier and submission details. Duplicates, and applications from calls that didn't come through NetEnroll, aren't billable.
Is there a monthly fee or a platform fee?
No monthly subscription and no charge for account access. You're billed per submitted application at the published rate for that program.
Which programs can I receive?
Final Expense, Medicare and ACA Health. Turn on the ones you're licensed for and choose the states you want calls from.
Can an agency run this for multiple producers?
Yes. An agency account can enroll its producers. The agency is responsible for their licensing and is billed for the applications submitted under its account.
Where do the calls come from?
Consumers who called in looking for coverage. Each call is routed live to an available producer matched to the consumer's state.
How do I get started?
Open an account in the agent portal, choose your programs and states, and take the first call. Get started
What if I think an application was billed in error?
Tell us within 30 days with the submission details and we investigate. The billing rules are written into the Terms, not just this page.
