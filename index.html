What is a CTV call?

CTV is connected TV: the ads people see while streaming on a television. Our Medicare and Final Expense ads run there with a phone number on screen. A CTV call is a viewer dialing that number.

Are these live calls or leads?

Live calls. The consumer dials in response to the ad and is connected to your agent while they are on the line. These are not lead records, lists or callback requests.

How does the 120-second billing buffer work?

A call that ends before 120 seconds is not billable. A call that reaches 120 seconds is billed at $39 for Medicare or $44 for Final Expense.

What licenses do our agents need?

Medicare: an active health license in the caller’s state, plus the carrier appointments and certifications required for Medicare sales. Final Expense: an active life insurance license in the caller’s state, plus the carrier appointments needed to write the business.

Which states can we get calls from?

Coverage depends on where campaigns air and where your agents are licensed. Send us your licensed states and we will confirm coverage.

Is call volume guaranteed?

No. Volume depends on airtime, time of day and the states your agents can take.
