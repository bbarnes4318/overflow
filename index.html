The 120-second buffer

Under two minutes? Not billable.

Every TV call carries a 120-second buffer. Hang-ups, misdials and quick exits that end before the two-minute mark aren’t billed. Once a call reaches 120 seconds, it’s billable at the published rate.

Questions

Questions, answered.

Something else? Call 904-512-8487.

What’s the difference between the two programs?

Pay per application: your producers take live calls and you pay the published program rate only when a qualifying application is submitted. The call itself is $0.

Pay per live inbound call: consumers call in from our television advertising and are routed live to your licensed agents. Calls that reach 120 seconds are billed at a flat $39 (Medicare) or $44 (Final Expense).

How does the 120-second buffer work on TV calls?

Calls shorter than 120 seconds are not billable. A TV call that reaches 120 seconds is billed at the program’s flat rate: $39 for Medicare, $44 for Final Expense.

On the pay-per-application program, do I pay for the call?

No. On that program NetEnroll does not charge you for connecting the consumer, the length of the conversation or a call that doesn’t result in an application. You pay the published program rate when a qualifying application is submitted.

What are the rates?

Pay per live inbound call: Medicare TV $39 per billable call, Final Expense TV $44 per billable call, each with a 120-second buffer.

Pay per application: Final Expense $199, Medicare $159, ACA Health $100 per submitted application.

Is there a monthly fee?

On the pay-per-application program there is no monthly subscription or per-producer seat fee. For TV inbound calls, account and billing setup is confirmed with our team before calls begin.

Where do the consumers come from?

TV inbound calls come from our television advertising: a consumer sees the ad and calls the number on screen.

On the pay-per-application program, consumers call looking for insurance coverage and NetEnroll routes those live conversations to available producers based on the programs and states you’ve enabled.

Can I choose my states?

Yes. On the application program you control the programs and states your account receives. For TV calls, tell us where your agents are licensed and our team will confirm coverage.

Do you guarantee a certain number of calls?

No. Consumer demand and airtime vary by program, state and time of day. What we can tell you upfront is exactly what a billable event costs.

What happens if a policy doesn’t take effect?

On the pay-per-application program, if an application qualifies under the applicable replacement terms, NetEnroll provides the corresponding replacement application according to those terms. See Terms §5.5
