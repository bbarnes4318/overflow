Questions

What producers ask before the first application.

What am I paying for?

A submitted application. A charge is logged only when your producer completes and submits a carrier application for a consumer NetEnroll delivered. Calls, transfers, non-qualifying prospects and dropped calls are $0.

What does a call cost if it doesn't become an application?

Nothing. There is no per-call, per-transfer or per-lead charge. If the conversation does not end in a submitted application, no charge is logged.

How does the application model work?

You fund applications in advance through Stripe, starting with a single one. Live consumers are routed to your producers in the states you selected. When an application is submitted, one is drawn from your balance. Buy more whenever you want them. There is no subscription.

What happens after I sign up?

Create your account at agents.netenroll.com, choose the states you are licensed to write in, choose your programs, and fund your first application. Routing starts once your account is configured and your states are mapped.

Can agencies use NetEnroll?

Yes. Register as a company, add your producers, and route by licensed state across the roster. Every application is recorded against the producer and the call it came from.

How does pricing work?

$199 per submitted final expense application, $125 for Medicare, $100 for ACA. No setup fee, no monthly platform fee, no minimum purchase and no cancellation penalty. The price is per application, not "from".

Where do I access my agent tools?

At agents.netenroll.com. That is where your states, programs, call log, applications and billing live. This site holds no session and never collects a credential.
