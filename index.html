What changes for you

The only call you pay for is the one you chose to write.

Billing at the application isn't a pricing detail. It changes what a bad call costs, how you test a source, and how far a budget goes.

  1. A bad call costs you time, not money.

    The wrong number, the not-eligible, the tire-kicker: all $0. Wasted spend stops at zero because the call was never the thing you were buying.

  2. You work the call before you pay for it.

    You've heard the consumer, qualified them and completed the application before a dollar is owed. You never pay for a stranger.

  3. Try a new source without funding a campaign.

    Turn on one program in one state. No monthly commitment, no block of calls to buy. The test costs the applications it produces, and nothing else.

  4. The rate is printed, not negotiated.

    $199, $125 or $100, the same for every call in the program, confirmed at checkout and written into the Terms. Your cost per application is fixed before the first call.

  5. A live consumer who called in about coverage.

    Not a list, not a form fill from last week. A person on the line who reached out about Final Expense, Medicare or ACA Health, routed to you while they're still on it.

  6. Ten producers cost the same as one, until they submit.

    One agency account, your producers under it, and no seat licenses. The bill grows only with applications, not with headcount.

Before you open an account

What producers ask us first.

When do I actually get charged?
When your producer submits an application for a consumer who came in on a NetEnroll call. That's the only event that costs anything. The charge is the published program rate ($199, $125 or $100) and it's recorded against your account with the carrier and submission details.
So if the call goes nowhere, I owe nothing?
Nothing. Not for the call, not for the minutes, not for the consumer who says no. A call without a submitted application never shows up on your bill.
Is there a monthly fee, a platform fee or a per-seat fee?
No. No subscription, no charge for account access and no per-producer fee. You pay per submitted application at the published rate for that program.
Where do the calls come from?
Consumers who called in looking for coverage: Final Expense, Medicare or ACA Health. Each call is routed live to an available producer matched to the consumer's state.
Can I choose which programs and states I take calls for?
Yes. Turn on the programs you're licensed for and choose the states you want calls from. Calls route to producers matched to the consumer's state.
Can I run this across my whole agency?
Yes. An agency account enrolls its producers under it. The agency is responsible for their licensing and is billed for the applications submitted under its account.
Do you guarantee call volume?
No. Volume depends on consumer demand in the programs and states you turn on. What's guaranteed in writing is the price: $0 for the call, the published rate for the application.
What if the policy never takes effect?
If the applicant never pays the first premium and you earn no commission, we credit your account with a replacement application on the same program at no charge. Tell us within 60 days with the submission details and the carrier's documentation. One replacement per application. Terms §5.5
What if I think I was billed for something I shouldn't have been?
Tell us within 30 days with the policy or submission number and applicant details and we investigate. Duplicates, applications that didn't come from a NetEnroll call, and contacts without consumer consent aren't billable under the Terms.
