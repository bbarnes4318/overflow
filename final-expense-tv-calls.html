Questions

Questions, answered.

Something else? Call 904-512-8487.

Where do the calls come from?

From television advertising. Consumers see a Final Expense ad and call the number on screen.

Are these live inbound calls?

Yes. The consumer places the call and is connected live to a participating agent. They are not lead records or callback lists.

How are calls billed?

$44 per billable call. Calls shorter than 120 seconds are not billable; a call that reaches 120 seconds is billed at $44. Funding and invoicing details are confirmed with you before calls start.

How does routing work?

Calls are routed to participating licensed agents according to the routing criteria agreed for your account. Agencies, telesales teams and call centers can route across multiple agents.

What licensing is required?

Agents must hold an active life insurance license in the caller’s state and the carrier appointments needed to write the business.

Who can participate?

Licensed independent agents, Final Expense agencies, telesales teams and insurance call centers. Participation is confirmed with our team before calls begin.

How does onboarding work?

Request access. We confirm your licensed states, routing and billing details with you, then turn calls on.

Is call availability guaranteed?

No. Volume depends on airtime, time of day and the states your agents can take. Ask our team about current availability for your states.

Can we start with a test?

Yes. Tell us about your team in the form below and we’ll talk through a starting volume.
