Are these live calls?

Yes. The consumer calls in response to a Medicare TV ad and is connected live to a participating agent. These are not lead records or callback requests.

How does the 120-second billing buffer work?

Calls shorter than 120 seconds are not billable. A call that reaches 120 seconds is billed at $39.

What licensing do our agents need?

An active health license in the caller’s state, plus the carrier appointments and certifications required for Medicare sales.

Which states are available?

Coverage depends on where campaigns air and where your agents are licensed. Send us your licensed states and we’ll confirm coverage.

Is call volume guaranteed?

No. Volume depends on airtime, time of day and the states your agents can take.
