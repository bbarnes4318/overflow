How it works

From airtime to answer in three steps.

The ad

Consumers see our television advertising.

We buy and run the airtime. The ad gives Medicare-eligible viewers a clear reason to call.

The call

They initiate an inquiry about Medicare coverage.

The consumer dials. No list, no callback, no cold open.

The connection

Calls route to participating licensed agents.

Routing follows the criteria agreed for your account, so calls reach agents who can take them.

Questions

Questions, answered.

Something else? Call 904-512-8487.

Where do the calls originate?

From television advertising. Consumers see a Medicare ad and call the number on screen.

Are these truly live inbound calls?

Yes. The consumer places the call and is connected live to a participating agent. These are not lead records, data lists or callback requests.

How does the buffer work?

Calls shorter than 120 seconds are not billable. Once a call reaches 120 seconds, it is billed at $39.

Are there licensing requirements?

Yes. Agents taking calls must hold an active health license in the caller’s state and meet the carrier appointment and certification requirements that apply to Medicare sales.

Is call availability guaranteed?

No. Call volume depends on airtime, time of day and the states your agents can take. Ask our team about current availability for your states.

Can agencies route calls to multiple agents?

Yes. Agencies and call centers can have calls routed across multiple licensed agents. Routing is set up with our team before calls begin.

What states are supported?

Coverage depends on where campaigns air and where your agents are licensed. Tell us your licensed states and we’ll confirm coverage.

How are calls billed?

$39 per billable call, meaning a call that reaches 120 seconds. Funding and invoicing details are confirmed with you before calls start.

Can we discuss a test?

Yes. Tell us about your team in the form below and we’ll talk through a starting volume.

How do we get started?

Request access. We confirm your licensed states, routing and agent setup with you, then turn calls on.
