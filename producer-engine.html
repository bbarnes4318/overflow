Everything that comes with your calls

Every tool below reads from the call itself. That's why it can do what a separate app can't.

So you're ready the moment they say hello

  • Warm Card: the caller's qualifying answers and their first words on your screen as the call connects.
  • Whisper Brief: a five-second audio summary of the caller before the line opens.
  • Caller Memory: when a caller calls back, they reach you, not a stranger.

So you write the app right the first time

  • Underwriting Copilot: health and medication answers from the call fill your underwriting questions.
  • Carrier Match: carriers ranked by approval odds, filtered to the carriers you're appointed with.
  • NetEnroll Quote Engine: quotes on the same screen as the call, with age and state already filled in.

So you get better every week

  • CloseCoach score on every call, with two specific fixes.
  • Weekly coaching digest: your calls, your scores and the one thing to work on next.
  • Rehearsal Room: practice against an AI caller and get scored the same way as live calls.

So the policy stays on the books

  • Draft-Day Guard: reminders to your client before the first draft, and a rescue call or text if a payment fails.
  • Policy and draft-date tracker for every policy you write.

So you know it's working

  • True Cost Ledger: your cost per submitted application and per policy still in force, updated live.
  • CRM and softphone: every call lands in your CRM already filled in. Take calls from your browser or phone.

So you never pay for what you didn't get

  • Transfer Rescue: miss a call and it's held and rerouted. You aren't billed.
  • Away Mode: one switch stops delivery and billing until you're back.
  • Proof-of-Call Ledger: recording, transcript and the caller's answers on every billed call, with one-click dispute.
  • ShieldLine: every call passes a litigator scrub and a calling-hours check before it reaches you.
